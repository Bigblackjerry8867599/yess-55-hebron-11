Our Story

From one small hotel in Kampala to a trusted name in hospitality

Genius Hospitality Group started in 2018 with one idea: travellers in Uganda deserved hotels that were clean, comfortable, affordable — and genuinely welcoming.

We opened our first property in Kampala with just 18 rooms and a small team who believed service matters more than luxury labels. Word spread. Business travellers came back. Families came for weekends. Couples chose us for weddings.

Today we run hotels in Kampala, Entebbe and Jinja — each with its own character, but the same promise: a good night’s sleep, a good breakfast, and staff who remember your name.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every hotel, every room, every guest

Warm Hospitality

We greet every guest like family — with a smile, a welcome drink, and help with bags, directions and plans.

Comfort First

Clean rooms, comfortable beds, hot showers, fast Wi-Fi and quiet nights. The basics, done brilliantly.

Prime Locations

Our hotels are close to business districts, airports, lakes and attractions — so you spend less time travelling.

Safe & Secure

24-hour reception, secure parking, CCTV and trained staff so you and your family feel safe.

Sustainable Growth
